# Week 3 – Logistics Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on advanced data analysis and visualization of logistics and supply chain data using Python. The objective is to explore delivery performance, sales patterns, shipping behaviour, profitability, and relationships between important numerical variables.

The analysis was performed using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) techniques and multiple visualizations to identify meaningful patterns and generate actionable business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyse logistics and supply chain data using Python
- Understand sales and benefit distributions
- Analyse delivery status and shipping performance
- Compare actual and scheduled shipping days
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Study relationships between important numerical variables
- Generate business-oriented insights and recommendations

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Delivery Status Analysis
Analysed the distribution of orders across different delivery statuses, including late delivery, advance shipping, shipping on time, and shipping cancelled.

### 2. Sales Performance by Product Category
Identified the top product categories based on total sales and compared their contribution to overall sales.

### 3. Shipping Performance Analysis
Compared average shipping time across different shipping modes to understand delivery performance.

### 4. Actual vs Scheduled Shipping Days
Compared scheduled shipping days with actual shipping days using a scatter plot to identify differences in planned and actual delivery performance.

### 5. Sales Distribution
Used a histogram to analyse how sales values are distributed across different orders.

### 6. Benefit per Order Distribution
Analysed the distribution of benefit per order to understand profitability and identify orders with positive and negative benefits.

### 7. Correlation Analysis
Used a correlation heatmap to study relationships between important numerical variables such as sales, product price, shipping days, benefit per order, and sales per customer.

## Key Findings

- Late delivery represents a significant proportion of orders.
- Sporting Goods recorded the highest sales among the top product categories.
- Same Day shipping had the lowest average shipping time.
- Actual shipping time does not always exactly match scheduled shipping time.
- Most orders generated positive benefits, although some orders resulted in negative benefits.
- Sales values were distributed across a wide range, with many orders concentrated in lower and middle sales ranges.
